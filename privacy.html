# Privacy Policy **Last Updated: October 16, 2026** ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how Orbs Bot handles information while providing task tracking and notification services on Discord. ## 2. Information We Process To provide its functionality, the bot may process limited Discord-related information, such as: - Discord server IDs - Discord channel IDs - Bot configuration and notification preferences - Publicly available Orbs task information The bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information is used solely to operate the bot, monitor Orbs-related tasks, deliver notifications, and maintain service functionality. ## 4. Data Sharing We do not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed through Discord and third-party services necessary for the bot's operation. ## 5. Data Retention Configuration information may be retained while the bot is active in a server or as necessary for its operation. Data that is no longer needed may be deleted. ## 6. Data Security Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. ## 7. User Rights Users and server administrators may contact the bot developer to request information about stored data or request its deletion, where applicable. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Changes will become effective when the revised policy is published. ## 9. Contact For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, please contact the bot developer through the bot's official Discord support channel.
