Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbs Bot handles information while providing task tracking and notification services on Discord.

2. Information We Process

The bot may process limited information such as:

  • Discord channel IDs
  • Bot configuration and notification preferences
  • Public Orbs task information

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the bot, track Orbs-related tasks, and deliver notifications.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Limited data may be processed through Discord and services required for bot functionality.

5. Data Retention

Configuration data may be retained while the bot is active in a server or as necessary for operation.

6. Data Security

Reasonable security measures are taken to protect information processed by the bot.

7. User Rights

Users may contact the developer to request information about their data or request deletion where applicable.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the bot developer through the official Discord support channel.
